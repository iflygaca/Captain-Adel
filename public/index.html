How it works

Retrieval, then read. Citation, then trust.

  1. 01

    You ask

    Plain English, plain Arabic, or a mix. "What are the VFR weather minima in controlled airspace?" works as well as "ما هي حدود الطقس للطيران البصري؟".

  2. 02

    Retrieval

    A lexical (BM25) search runs over the indexed GACAR corpus — currently 47,361 chunks across GACAR Parts 1 through 199, the topical handbooks, and AIP excerpts.

  3. 03

    Read

    The model is handed the retrieved passages under a strict cite-or-refuse contract. For critical limits (minima, distances, weights) it quotes the regulation verbatim.

  4. 04

    Verify

    Every answer ends with a three-state grounding badge (Grounded / Partial / Hold) and a Verify against the official source line.

Q

What are the VFR weather minima in controlled airspace?

Grounded 91.155

Short answer: In controlled airspace below 10,000 ft MSL, the GACAR basic VFR weather minima are 1,000 ft ceiling and 3 sm visibility (Class B, C, D, E).

Detail:

  • Airspace class B: 3 sm visibility; clear of clouds.
  • Class C, D, E (below 10,000 ft MSL): 3 sm visibility, 500 ft below clouds, 1,000 ft above, 2,000 ft horizontal.
  • Class E (at or above 10,000 ft MSL): 5 sm visibility, 1,000 ft below, 1,000 ft above, 1 sm horizontal.

Cite: GACAR Part 91, §91.155 — Basic VFR weather minimums.

See also: §91.167 (Special VFR) · §91.169 (VFR flight plan).

Source (verbatim)

§ 91.155 — Basic VFR weather minimums. Except as provided in §91.167 of this part, the following weather minimums apply to VFR flight operations: (a) Airspace class B — visibility 3 sm, clear of clouds. (b) Airspace class C, D, or E (below 10,000 ft MSL) — visibility 3 sm; 500 ft below clouds; 1,000 ft above clouds; 2,000 ft horizontal from clouds…
