VFR weather minima in controlled airspace, below 10,000 ft

In controlled airspace below 10,000 ft MSL, VFR requires 3 SM flight visibility and cloud clearance of 500 ft below, 1,000 ft above, 2,000 ft horizontal.

This is the controlled-airspace case; uncontrolled and higher-altitude classes differ.
  • Flight visibility & distance-from-cloud are set in .
  • Special VFR and the <3 SM exceptions are carved out in .

Regulation reads — §91.155(a)

“…no person may operate an aircraft under VFR when the flight visibility is less, or at a distance from clouds that is less, than that prescribed… 3 statute miles; 500 ft below, 1,000 ft above, 2,000 ft horizontal.”

max crosswind for a student pilot on a solo flight?

I can't verify that figure. There is no single GACAR number for a student's solo crosswind limit — it's set by your instructor's endorsement and the AFM, not by a regulation.

Why this is a hold, not a dodge: a wrong limit is more dangerous than no limit. Student solo currency lives in Part 61; the actual crosswind ceiling is the lower of your AFM demonstrated value and your instructor's limitation.
